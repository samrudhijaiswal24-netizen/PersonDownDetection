Verified Root Causes & System Limitations

mcfd_chute14_cam1.avi RESOLVED (Phase 6)

Resolution: Foreshortened diagonal corner perspective previously caused a 55° 2D torso angle and bent knees, falsely boosting sitting (+0.35) over lying. Adding foreshortened lying rules and unconditioned knee flexion suppression recovered detection at frame 1133 (delay: 2.97s).

mcfd_chute02_cam1.avi OCCLUSION CONSTRAINT

Hardware Limitation: In 198 of 201 ground-truth fall frames (98.5%), the subject fell completely behind high furniture, leaving zero visible body keypoints or bounding boxes. Preserved honestly as camera occlusion constraint without synthetic fabrication.

mcfd_chute16_cam1.avi DETECTOR CONFIDENCE LIMIT

Model Limitation: Subject confidence during fall dropped to ~0.13 (below 0.50 threshold), while an office chair with coat was detected as an upright person (conf 0.801). Preserved honestly to prevent lowering thresholds that would cause false alarms.

🏥 Safe Resting Policy & Collapse Detection Logic

SAFE RESTING (BED / SOFA)

When a person lies down in bed_sofa_zone, the system classifies the state as BED_SOFA_LYING. The floor timer remains at 0.0s and no alerts are triggered.

COLLAPSE CANDIDATE (FLOOR ZONE)

When a person lies down in floor_zone or floor_mat, the system accumulates the floor down timer. If evidence ratio ≥ 65% over 1.5s, a candidate event is confirmed.

UPRIGHT RECOVERY (N=1)

If the subject stands up or sits upright, a single frame confirmation immediately resets the candidate timer, preventing spurious continuation.
